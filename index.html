Why Italy?

Respected degrees

Gateway to Europe

Italy is fast becoming a top choice for Indian students

Indian students enrolled at Italian universities, as stated by Italy's ambassador to India in October 2024. Bars start at zero.

What officials and reports say

  • 2021/22 and 2022/23: Ministry of Labour reports based on MUR data count 5,260 and 5,196 Indian students enrolled at universities. 2022 report, 2023 report
  • Oct 2024: Italy's ambassador to India said Indian students enrolled in Italian universities rose from 250 to about 12,000 in ten years, with 2,500 new enrolments in the past academic year. ANSA

Italy funds international students through regional grants, government scholarships and EU programmes. Here are the amounts published for 2026/27, converted into Indian rupees.

DSU: grants from Italian regions, based on family income. MAECI: scholarships from Italy’s foreign ministry. Invest Your Talent: an Italian government programme for Master’s students. ISEE: the income form used to work out DSU.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to speak Italian?

Not for English-taught programs, but Italian helps daily life and some universities ask for it. Always check each program's language requirements.

Which visa do I need?

Non-EU students typically apply for a national long-stay student visa through the Italian embassy or consulate, after pre-enrolment on Universitaly. Check current rules on the official Italian foreign ministry site.

Are scholarships available for Indian students?

Yes. Options include regional right-to-study (DSU) scholarships, university merit awards and government programs. Eligibility and deadlines change every year.

Can I work while studying?

Student visa holders may be allowed limited part-time work. Confirm the current weekly and annual limits before planning your budget.

What marks or test scores do I need?

Requirements depend on the university and programme. Many ask for a minimum grade in your previous degree, and some ask for IELTS or similar. Others accept alternatives or do not require a test. We check this for each shortlisted programme.

Do I need an entrance test for medicine?

English-taught medicine programmes generally require an entrance exam such as the IMAT. Rules change each year, so confirm the current one on the official Ministry and university pages.

How long does the whole process take?

Plan for several months from first application to visa approval. We recommend starting 8 to 10 months before your intake, because deadlines, scholarship portals and visa appointments all have fixed dates.

What if I am not eligible for a scholarship?

We tell you honestly at the free assessment. Eligibility depends on your documents, income and the region. If you do not qualify for one scheme, we look at others or at lower-cost universities. We never promise an award.

What does the visa process cost me besides your fee?

You pay the visa fee and VFS charges directly, and you must show proof of funds as required by the consulate. See the section above for what is paid separately.
